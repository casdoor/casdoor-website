Casdoor is free and open source under Apache-2.0, and every feature, including enterprise SSO, SCIM, MFA and Casbin authorization, is in the open-source edition. Commercial support is a service, not a feature gate.

Casbin offers annual support contracts for self-hosted Casdoor and for [Casdoor Cloud](https://www.casdoor.com/pricing). They are for teams that need a support line, documents for a procurement or security review, or help with a migration or a high-availability rollout.

## What a contract covers

| Area | What you get |
|------|--------------|
| Response times | 8x5 support with a one-business-day first response. Production-down incidents get a four-business-hour response, and extended hours or a named engineer can be added to the contract. |
| Security and procurement | Security questionnaire, an SBOM generated from the release build, the vulnerability handling and disclosure process, NDA, DPA and the vendor forms your procurement team needs. |
| High availability and upgrades | Review of clustering and session design, rolling upgrades, backup and restore drills, and a plan for upgrading without downtime. |
| Migration | Moving from Keycloak, Auth0, Okta, Azure AD B2C or a custom IAM: user and password-hash migration, parallel running and a cutover plan. |
| LTS and backports | A designated release line for your deployment, with security fixes backported and advance notice of breaking changes. |
| Contract and billing | Annual contract, invoice or purchase order, named contacts on both sides, and renewal planning. |

## How it works

1. Email [support@casbin.com](mailto:support@casbin.com) with your deployment model, number of users and applications, compliance requirements and timeline.
2. You get a written proposal with scope, response times and price, usually within two business days.
3. Onboarding starts with a call to agree on named contacts, a private support channel and a review of your deployment.

For managed hosting, Casdoor Cloud offers self-serve plans and an enterprise option with an uptime SLA; see [pricing](https://www.casdoor.com/pricing) and [enterprise support](https://www.casdoor.com/enterprise).
